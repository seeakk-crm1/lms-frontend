SEEAKK – Sales Execution & Accountability Platform with CRM

From Sales Targets to Sales Execution. Stop losing leads. Start building accountability.

SEEAKK helps business owners and sales heads see who owns each lead, which follow-ups are due or overdue, which stage approvals are pending, how staff are progressing against targets, and why opportunities were lost.

What SEEAKK helps with

  • Lead management: assignment, bulk assignment, ownership and lifecycle history
  • Follow-up management: schedule, reschedule, snooze and complete calls, meetings and visits
  • Configurable stages, stage rules and manager approvals
  • Target cycles and progress tracking for staff and teams
  • Revenue reporting and Loss of Business (LOB) analysis
  • Roles and permissions, activity reports, attendance and calendar

Who it is for

Sales teams with recurring enquiries and multi-step follow-up, such as study-abroad consultancies, real estate developers, solar providers, interiors and construction firms, and automobile dealerships.
